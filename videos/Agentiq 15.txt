The two biggest reasons small businesses won't go further with AI are data security
and not trusting its accuracy. Both of those are solvable, not permanent blockers.
About a third of small businesses cite each one as a barrier and honestly they're right to be cautious.
Generic AI tools do have both problems.

instead of handing it to a public module. Accuracy gets solved by grounding the AI
in your own documents and systems instead of letting it guess. Both of those are just
