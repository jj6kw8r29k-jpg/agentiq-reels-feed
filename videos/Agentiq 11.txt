AI chip stocks just had one of their worst weeks ever. That doesn't mean AI is a bubble
about to pop. Micron dropped 13% in a single day. Intel fell over 20% in a week. Meanwhile,
TSMC just committed another 100 billion dollars to its US operations on top of what they'd
already promised. Stockswings are traders reacting to short-term expectations. That 100 billion
dollars is a company betting on real sustained demand for years. Don't confuse a volatile
week in the market with whether now's the right time to build for your own business.
