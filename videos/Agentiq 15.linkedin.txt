The two biggest reasons small businesses won't go further with AI: data security and not trusting its accuracy. About a third of small businesses cite each one as a barrier, and honestly, they're right to be cautious. Generic AI tools do have both problems.

But both are solvable, not permanent blockers.

Data security gets solved by controlling where your data actually lives instead of handing it to a public model. Accuracy gets solved by grounding the AI in your own documents and systems instead of letting it guess.

Both of those are just architecture decisions, not unsolvable problems.
